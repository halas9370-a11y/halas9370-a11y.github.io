01 — WHO WE ARE

Ideas become
experiments.

HalaLoya brings together interdisciplinary ideas and research questions across artificial intelligence, human-centered technology and emerging scientific fields. The goal is to explore how different technologies can connect, how research questions can become experimental concepts, and how promising ideas may eventually lead toward practical technological possibilities.

02 — OUR APPROACH

Research before
assumption.

HalaLoya approaches emerging technology as a process of exploration, experimentation and validation. Research concepts are presented as research concepts. Scientific and technological claims require evidence, testing and interdisciplinary validation before conclusions are drawn.

04 — PRINCIPLES

Innovation with
responsibility.

Technology should expand human capability without removing human agency. Responsible development, privacy, transparency and meaningful human oversight are therefore part of the HalaLoya approach.

Human-Centered Technology should serve meaningful human needs.
Responsible Innovation should consider safety, privacy and societal impact.
Evidence-Based Research claims should be tested and validated.
Open to Exploration Emerging ideas deserve investigation without premature conclusions.
05 — THE VISION

Build what is
worth exploring.

The long-term vision of HalaLoya is to explore the space between scientific curiosity and technological possibility. By connecting AI with emerging fields and human-centered innovation, HalaLoya aims to investigate ideas that may contribute to new ways of understanding, interacting with and building technology.
