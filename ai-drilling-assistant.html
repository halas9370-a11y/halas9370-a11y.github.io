HalaLoya AI Drilling Assistant

A proposed decision-support system that starts with pre-drilling sample and geological assessment, then monitors available drilling data to help engineers identify potential risks earlier.

Current status: research concept only. No integrated prototype, field trial, prediction accuracy or operational performance has been validated.

Conceptual architecture only. It does not represent a tested product or a claim of measured performance.

How the proposed system could work

1. Before drilling

Review soil or rock sample results, geological information and permitted records from nearby wells. A surface soil sample alone cannot establish conditions at deep drilling intervals.

2. During drilling

Where authorized data access exists, ingest measurements such as rate of penetration, weight on bit, torque, pressure, and relevant drilling-fluid or flow data.

3. Risk-specific analysis

Develop and validate separate models for a defined event, beginning with a candidate such as lost-circulation risk if suitable historical data can be obtained.

4. Engineer-reviewed warning

Display the potential risk, supporting signals, uncertainty and data-quality limits. The experimental system remains advisory and does not control drilling equipment.

Candidate research scope

  • First candidate: early assessment of lost-circulation risk, subject to data availability and engineering review.
  • Potential later directions: indicators associated with wellbore instability and unusual operating trends, each requiring its own data and validation.
  • Nanotechnology is an optional research component only if a sensor demonstrably adds useful measurements compared with existing methods.

Proof-of-concept roadmap

  1. Secure permission to use anonymized historical well data with time, depth, operating measurements and labeled events.
  2. Define one target event and compare a simple baseline with a risk-specific machine-learning model.
  3. Test on wells excluded from training and report recall, precision, false alarms, warning lead time and uncertainty.
  4. Only after independent review consider a read-only connection to rig data; no control commands in the experimental prototype.
