HALALOYA — PROJECT 01

HALALOYA 40+

AI designed around the journey of women entering a new stage of life.

A human-centered AI platform exploring personalized interaction, memory, emotional intelligence, dreams and life journeys for women 40 and beyond.

← Back to HalaLoya
01 — THE IDEA

A different kind
of AI companion.

HalaLoya 40+ is an AI-centered platform concept designed to provide personalized support through a user's own memories, emotions, aspirations and life goals. Rather than treating every interaction as an isolated conversation, the system is designed to build continuity across the user's journey.

02 — THE AI SYSTEM

Intelligence built
around the journey.

The platform combines several AI-driven components designed to create a more continuous and personalized experience.

01 — MEMORY

AI Memory

A structured memory layer designed to organize confirmed and user-controlled memories, allowing the experience to become more personalized over time.

02 — EMOTION

Emotion Engine

An interaction layer that classifies emotional signals in user messages and can adapt the assistant's communication style.

03 — JOURNEY

Journey Intelligence

A system designed to combine memories, emotions, dreams, journaling and progress into personalized journey insights.

04 — DREAMS

Dream Board

Users can define dreams and goals, add target dates and notes, and receive AI-assisted planning and progress guidance.

05 — VOICE

Voice AI

Voice interaction designed to support Arabic speech-to-text, text-to-speech and conversational interaction.

06 — 90 DAYS

Journey Planning

A structured 90-day journey concept connecting goals, daily interactions and progress.

04 — RESPONSIBLE AI

Responsibility
by design.

Because HalaLoya 40+ works with personal information and AI-generated interactions, responsible AI principles are an important part of the product design.

Privacy
Respecting user control over personal information.
Transparency
Making AI interaction understandable to users.
User Control
Giving users control over memories and personal data.
Human Oversight
Keeping meaningful decisions under human control.
05 — DEVELOPMENT

From concept
to implementation.

Current Development

HalaLoya 40+ is an evolving technology project. Several platform components and AI interaction features are being developed and tested, while additional capabilities remain under research and development.
