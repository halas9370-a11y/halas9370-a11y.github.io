EXPLORATORY RESEARCH DIRECTION

AI Applications in Space Exploration

An emerging research and learning direction examining where AI may assist space-science workflows and autonomous systems. This is an exploratory topic page, not a completed research paper or a report of original results.

Exploratory research direction only. No completed manuscript, original experiment or validated system is claimed.

← Back to Research Portfolio
01 — ABSTRACT

Overview

Potential areas for investigation include scientific-image analysis, anomaly detection in spacecraft telemetry, mission planning under constraints, robotic autonomy and support for researchers working with large scientific datasets. Each use case presents distinct reliability, explainability, verification and operational-safety requirements. A credible research project would need to narrow this broad area to one defined problem, review existing work and evaluate a proposed method against relevant baselines.

02 — RESEARCH QUESTIONS

Questions to investigate

  • Which specific space-science or mission-support task is both meaningful and feasible to study?
  • What public datasets and established baselines could support a reproducible evaluation?
  • How should uncertainty, failure modes and human oversight be handled in safety-critical settings?

Keywords
