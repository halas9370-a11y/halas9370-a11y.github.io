02 — RESEARCH STANDARDS

Evidence before claims

Publication status: peer-reviewed publication, conference submission, working manuscript and concept are different categories. Each item should link to a DOI, official venue page, or documented status when available.


Data and methods: identify data sources, permissions, limitations and evaluation methods. Publicly accessible data is not automatically licensed for every reuse.


Responsible technology: explain uncertainty, potential harms and the need for expert review, especially in safety-sensitive or dual-use domains.
